Figures, Tables, and Apps

A consolidated index of every numbered figure and table in the book, plus the interactive Shiny apps. Click any entry to jump to it in context.

Interactive apps

  • What goes wrong (in Optimization for Maximum Likelihood Estimation) — choose a starting value for Newton-Raphson on a logistic log-likelihood and step through the iterations, watching the method converge or run away.
  • Interactive demonstration (in EM Algorithms) — run the EM algorithm on a two-component normal mixture from a chosen starting point and step through the E- and M-steps.
  • Explore it interactively (in Introduction to Bayesian Inference) — pick a conjugate model and its prior, generate data, and watch the posterior update.
  • When the grid misses the posterior’s mode (in Numerical Quadrature) — move a Gaussian posterior’s mode and see the fixed midpoint grid miss it entirely.
  • Adaptive Gauss–Hermite Quadrature (in Laplace Approximation) — compare the Laplace approximation against adaptive Gauss-Hermite quadrature and Monte Carlo as a posterior’s shape changes.
  • Trying it interactively: tight vs. loose envelopes (in Rejection Sampling) — tune the tightness of a Laplace envelope around a standard normal target and watch the acceptance rate respond.
  • Trying it interactively: the Cauchy envelope (in Rejection Sampling) — tune a Cauchy envelope against a Gamma target and see acceptance collapse as the shape parameter moves away from the tuned point.
  • Animation for Illustrating ARS (in Rejection Sampling) — watch adaptive rejection sampling build and refine its piecewise-linear hull around a log-concave density.
  • Explore it interactively (in Importance Sampling) — move a normal proposal relative to a mixture target and watch the effective sample size and estimate quality respond.
  • Explore it interactively (in Markov Chain Monte Carlo) — tune the step size and number of leapfrog steps of Hamiltonian Monte Carlo on a correlated target and compare against random-walk Metropolis.
